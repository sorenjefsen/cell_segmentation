Purpose:
DNA gets damaged often, but will get repaired when the cell is not undergoing mitosis. If mitosis is in process, the cell has these other emergency functions that allows DNA repair. Cancer cells are constantly going through mitosis, so here these other repair effects are crucial for the survival of the cell. 

He is working with a protein called top-BP1. It is always present in cells, but a bit before, during and after mitosis, it is present at a higher concentration. Its primary function is to hold the two divided cells together, to allow other proteins to do some DNA repair. It also has other functions. 

During the mitosis, it is mostly positioned right on the boundary where the splitting is happening. 

If the top-BP1 is inhibited in some way, the DNA repair is very low.
